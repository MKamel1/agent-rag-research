## Instrument assessment
Vocabulary is moderately diverse (14 distinct terms in 26 items) but concentrated: instrument (4), matching (3), interference (3), and discontinuity, shock, causal attention, control (2 each). The traps are mostly contrived: distractors were found by term occurrence anywhere in the 400k chunks, not by closeness to the question, and 18 questions never type the term, so the set mostly cannot lure a retriever. It would detect a retriever that suppresses legitimate off-topic answers (the 14 offtopic-legit items are good for that) and one that fails absent-handling on name-only citations (041-045); it would miss on-topic-but-wrong-paper retrieval for generic facts (RD, IV, collider) and chunk-level misses among overlapping chunks (4, 16, 20, 21, 23 have second gold chunks), and the absurd absent items (047, 048) test nothing.
