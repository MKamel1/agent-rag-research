# Acquisitions list: what the standards corpus lacks or holds only in part

Sources: the author-E "expected-but-missing" list, re-verified for CQ-5 by reading all 119 titles and
searching every chunk for each designation. "Cited only" means the designation appears in other
documents' text but no document of that name is held. No URLs are given because none were verified.
Obtain copies from the issuing body or a national standards body's store; free regulations from the
regulator's official publication service.
